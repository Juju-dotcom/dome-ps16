# 1. Printing

Print settings and quantities for every part are in [hardware/README.md](../hardware/README.md).
This page covers the order and what to check when a plate comes off the printer.

## Order

1. **Two ASA test corners** (`hardware/stl/test/`), about 50 minutes. Check that the lip and rabbet fit without forcing, the insert
   holes measure about 4.0 mm, and an insert set at 230 °C goes in straight and flush.
2. **First PETG-CF parts**: one LED capsule, one hub, the motor plate, one foot and the camera
   tower. Check that a LED star fits in the capsule (4 pegs in the 4 notches), the motor screws
   line up with the plate, and the camera slides into the tower.
3. **The rest of the PETG-CF**: capsules, hubs, rim hubs, adapters, top cap, feet. Dry the
   filament overnight first.
4. **ASA dome panels** 00–19 (no supports), then 20–39 (tree supports). Print the turntable
   with the first plates, the base needs it early.
5. **ASA skirt, towers and console parts.**
6. **Enclosure last**, after test-fitting the real electronics (jack, USB, DB37, switch)
   against the holes.
7. **PLA calibration fixtures** whenever, they're only needed at the end.

## Checks on every plate

- No warped corners, the part sits flat on the table.
- Lips and rabbets are clean: run a fingernail along them, nothing should catch. Remove the
  brim without touching the 1 mm lips.
- Insert holes 4.0 ± 0.2 mm. If they're off by more, fix the print before setting 159 inserts.
- Supports come off without leaving a bump on a mating face.
